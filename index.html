Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know if Geometry Dash servers are down?

If the box above displays "Short Answer: Yes", the servers are currently unreachable. If it says "No", the servers are up, and the issue is likely on your end.

Why can't I connect to Geometry Dash?

Connection issues usually happen due to database maintenance, high player traffic, or local network problems. Check your internet connection if this tracker says servers are online.

Why am I getting a "Save Failed" error?

If the servers are online but you can't save your progress, your save file might be too heavy. Try deleting copyable levels, clearing your downloaded songs, and removing created levels from your list.

Is this an official RobTop status checker?

No, this is a lightweight, fan-made community tool. It checks the live status of the official boomlings.com server directly from your browser, independent of RobTop Games.
