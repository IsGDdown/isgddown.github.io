Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know if Geometry Dash servers are down?

If the status displays "Short Answer: Yes", the services monitored by isGDDown appear to be unavailable. If it displays "Short Answer: No", the checks indicate that they are reachable. This does not guarantee that every online feature is working.

How does isGDDown check server status?

The website requests a status report from its own backend. The backend checks the availability of selected Geometry Dash online services, including Boomlings connectivity and leaderboard availability. The result is based on those checks, not on an official RobTop Games status feed.

Why can't I connect to Geometry Dash?

Connection problems can have different causes, including server-side issues, internet connectivity, or problems with a particular online service. If isGDDown reports that its checks are successful, try checking your connection and testing again later.

Why am I getting a "Save Failed" error?

A save error does not necessarily mean that all Geometry Dash servers are down. It may have several causes. Check that your connection is working and consult reliable Geometry Dash support information if the problem continues.

No. isGDDown is an independent, fan-made project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by RobTop Games.
